# QRD - Quadratic Residue Diffuser

Appearance: Rows of narrow wells of varying depths.

- Material: Usually wood or MDF; rigidity matters more than material type.
- Typical depth: 10–20 cm as an example; depth depends on target frequency and sequence order.
- Effect: Scatters mid- and high-frequency reflections; usable range depends on well depth, width, and incidence angle.

Often found on the rear wall of professional recording studios and control rooms.
