# QBarrel / Curved Diffuser

A barrel- or dome-shaped diffuser made of slats (often QRD-based) arranged over a curved, convex surface.

- Slat depths can follow a QRD sequence, but the curvature itself also spreads reflections.
- Material: Wood, MDF, or plastic slats.
- Typical depth: 10–30 cm radial depth as an example; depth depends on design.
- Effect: Curved geometry spreads reflections; two-dimensional curvature may spread in both planes, simple cylindrical curvature primarily spreads in one plane.

Popular in studio live ends, rear walls, and control rooms because it handles a wide range of incidence angles better than flat panel diffusers. Curvature increases angular dispersion and helps maintain a more uniform response across incidence angles.
