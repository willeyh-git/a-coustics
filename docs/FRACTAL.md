# Fractal Diffuser

A diffuser whose surface height pattern is built from recursive (self-similar) sequences, e.g. fractal versions of a QRD or prime sequence, or a "stepped pyramid" made of scaled-up sub-patterns.

- Combines the scattering properties of a base sequence with additional depth variation at multiple scales.
- Material: Wood, MDF, or composite.
- Typical depth: 15–40 cm — the extra depth extends the usable diffusion range to lower frequencies.
- Effect: Broadband scattering; multi-scale patterns can extend usable range, but benefits depend on design and are not guaranteed.

Used where a strong, diffuse field over a wide band is wanted, e.g. vocal booths, live rooms, and large control rooms. Computationally, the pattern is often generated by repeating a base sequence at progressively larger block sizes.
