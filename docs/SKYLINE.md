## Skyline diffuser

- Appearance: A three-dimensional grid of wooden blocks with different heights.
- Material: Solid wood or MDF.
- Typical depth: 10–30 cm as an example; depth depends on design.
- Effect: Scatters sound in both horizontal and vertical directions; performance depends on block size, height distribution, and panel dimensions.

Popular for studio rear walls and listening rooms where broad, multidirectional scattering is desirable.
