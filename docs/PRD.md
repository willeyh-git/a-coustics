# PRD - Prime Residue Diffuser

A one-dimensional diffuser that scatters sound by reflecting from wells of varying depths.

- Well depths are derived from a primitive-root sequence (e.g. g^k mod p with p prime), which spreads the reflection phase more evenly than QRD.
- Material: Usually wood, MDF, or composite slabs.
- Typical depth: 10–25 cm (set by the lowest target frequency: d ≈ c / (4f)).
- Effect: Diffuses mid- and high-frequency energy in the horizontal plane.

Common in studio rear walls and home theater. Performance depends on sequence scaling and panel geometry; claims about lower pressure or reduced distortion are not established as general advantages.
