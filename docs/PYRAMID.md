# Pyramid Diffuser

A family of designs including solid raised pyramids, open-well pyramid grids, and hybrid absorber-diffusers.

- Material: Wood, MDF, plastic, or foam; open-backed versions provide some absorption at low frequencies.
- Typical depth: 5–15 cm.
- Effect: Breaks up high-frequency specular reflections; open wells can provide frequency-dependent resonant behavior, absorption depends on losses and geometry.

Simple and inexpensive. Mostly effective above the frequency where the well/well-spacing dimensions are comparable to the wavelength — not a true broadband diffuser. Common in project studios and as a low-cost treatment on rear walls and ceiling corners.
